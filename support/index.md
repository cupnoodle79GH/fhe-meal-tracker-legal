## Contact support

For help with FHE's Meal Tracker, email **cupnoodle79@gmail.com**.

Please include:

- A short description of what happened.
- The app version, build number, and environment shown under **Settings → App Settings**.
- Your iPhone model and iOS version.
- The approximate date and time of the problem.
- A screenshot, if it does not reveal information you do not want to share.

Do not send your password, authentication code, OpenAI key, Supabase secret, or complete medical records.

## Common questions

### AI analysis is slow or does not respond

Check that the phone has a stable internet connection and try again. Cloud AI analysis and the hosted QA service can occasionally take longer to start. Do not repeatedly tap the analysis button while a request is already running.

### A meal estimate is incorrect

Before saving, edit the meal description or open **AI refinement**, add the correction, and select **Regenerate with correction**. Nutrition values are estimates and should be reviewed before you save the meal.

### I cannot sign in

Check that you are using the correct email address. Use **Forgot password** on the sign-in screen to request a reset link. If no email arrives, check the spam or junk folder before contacting support.

### I want a copy of my data

Open **Settings → Profile → Account & Privacy**, then choose **Export My Data**. The exported file contains account-related application data. Private photograph links in an export may expire for security.

### I want to delete my account

Open **Settings → Profile → Account & Privacy**, then choose **Delete Account**. Follow the confirmation steps. Account deletion is permanent and includes your FHE profile, meals, weights, goals, photographs, saved meal locations, progress records, and local FHE cache, except information that must be retained by law.

If subscriptions are introduced later, deleting the FHE account will not automatically cancel an Apple subscription. Subscriptions must also be managed through the Apple Account subscription settings.

### How do I report a privacy or security concern?

Email **cupnoodle79@gmail.com** with the subject **Privacy request** or **Security report**. Do not include sensitive information beyond what is necessary to explain the concern.
